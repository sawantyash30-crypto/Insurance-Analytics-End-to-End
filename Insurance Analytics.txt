# Insurance Analytics – End-to-End Data Analytics Project

## Project Overview
This project provides a unified insurance analytics solution to track branch-level and individual performance across New, Cross-Sell, and Renewal business lines.

## Business Problem
The organization lacked a centralized view to track targets, achievements, opportunities, and conversion ratios across branches and account executives.

## Tools Used
- Excel (Data cleaning & KPI calculation)
- SQL (Data modeling & analysis)
- Power BI (Interactive dashboards & KPIs)
- Tableau (Executive storytelling dashboard)

## Key KPIs
- New / Cross-sell / Renewal Target vs Achievement
- Placed vs Invoiced Achievement
- Open Opportunity & Closed Won
- Conversion Ratio
- Meetings Count
- Branch & Individual Performance

## Dashboards
- Power BI: Executive, Branch, and Individual dashboards
- Tableau: Management-level performance view

## Business Impact
The dashboard enables real-time performance tracking, identifies top performers, highlights bottlenecks, and supports data-driven decision-making.
